Jack Larson

Formerly Lafayette College · Potomac, Maryland · Biology (Pre-Med)

Jack Larson (full name Jackson Larson) is a junior pole vaulter at Virginia Tech who transferred from Lafayette College in 2026, joining the Hokies in fall 2026.

At Lafayette, he broke the school's 42-year-old pole vault record in April 2026 with a clearance of 16'9.5" (5.12 m), and placed fourth at the 2025 Patriot League Indoor Championships. Before college, he attended Winston Churchill High School in Potomac, Maryland, where he set the school pole vault record, won county and 2024 Maryland 4A West Regional titles, was a three-time Maryland 4A state runner-up, and placed third at the 2024 Nike Indoor Nationals (Emerging Elite).

He was a Washington Post All-Met honorable mention in both indoor and outdoor track during the 2023–24 season.

He studies Biology on the pre-med track at Virginia Tech. At Lafayette, he earned a 3.95 cumulative GPA, made the Dean's List all four semesters, was named to the Patriot League Academic Honor Roll for the winter and spring seasons from 2024 to 2026, and was a member of Beta Beta Beta, the national biological honor society.
